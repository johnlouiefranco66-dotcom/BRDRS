How BRDRS Verification Works

1. Select your requested Barangay document and fill out the specific details required for that certificate.
2. Check only the supporting documents you are submitting and upload a clear photo or PDF.
3. Choose between Online Bank/E-Wallet or Walk-in Cash Payment.
4. Present your Official Receipt on the indicated Date of Releasing to claim your certificate.
